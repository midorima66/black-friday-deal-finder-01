How these prices were checked. Every deal on this page was read on Friday 27 November 2026 (UTC morning) from a deals roundup that names its store (9to5Toys, Mashable, TradingView) and its store link was followed to the store's own product page — or, for Best Buy doorbusters, to Best Buy's Black Friday sale page. The sale price is the price the store showed; the usual price is the store's own comparison price; the discount is worked out from those two. Prices move all day on Black Friday: check the price at checkout before you buy.

What the verdict means. NEW LOW — the roundup calls it an all-time or record low. STRONG — a steep cut of 24% or more. ROUTINE — a modest cut under 20%; fine if you needed it anyway, not a reason to buy.
